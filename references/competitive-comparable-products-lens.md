# Project Starter Kit: Competitive / Comparable Products Lens

## Purpose

Use this lens when public products, adjacent workflows, mature tools, or competitor positioning could change product discovery, feature selection, onboarding, pricing, trust language, UX patterns, or differentiation.

This is not a feature-copying step. It is a pattern-learning and differentiation step.

## Trigger Conditions

- The user asks for market examples, competitors, comparable products, or inspiration.
- The product is in a crowded or familiar category.
- The product depends on user trust, onboarding, pricing, habit formation, or a common workflow.
- The Product Design Gate uses a market/pattern scan and the scan materially affects the product.
- The first product noun is generic, such as app, dashboard, tracker, assistant, planner, marketplace, or platform.

## Source Guidance

Use current public sources when product positioning, pricing, features, or platform behavior may have changed. Prefer official product pages, app store pages, public help docs, credible case studies, and mature comparable tools. Do not treat marketing claims as proof of traction unless independently supported.

## Required Output

Record in `prototype-pass.md` or `project-brief.md`:

```text
Comparable products:
Comparison dimensions:
- positioning
- primary user
- core loop
- onboarding burden
- key features
- monetization
- trust/privacy language
- UX pattern
- what to borrow
- what to avoid
- differentiation implication
Feature decisions changed:
```

## Comparison Standard

Compare 3-5 products or patterns when available. If fewer are available, explain why.

For each comparable, identify:

- what job it appears to solve
- what its first-use or activation moment seems to be
- what it makes easy
- what it makes heavy or risky
- what vocabulary it normalizes
- what the new product should borrow, reject, or differentiate from

## Output Rule

The lens passes only when it changes or confirms at least one product decision:

- positioning
- MVP inclusion or exclusion
- onboarding approach
- trust/privacy copy
- monetization hypothesis
- UX pattern
- validation question

If nothing changes, record that the scan confirmed the existing direction and why.
