# Project Starter Kit: Business And Acquisition Gate

## Purpose

Use this gate when a product must attract, retain, or monetize users beyond a private tool or one-off prototype.

## Trigger Conditions

- Consumer product.
- Paid product or future monetization.
- Marketplace, community, social, creator, wellness, healthcare, fashion, education, productivity, or workflow product.
- Product depends on distribution, trust, referrals, repeat usage, or a specific buyer.
- Review identifies missing acquisition channel, activation event, retention loop, buyer, pricing, or willingness-to-pay evidence.

## Required Output

Add a "Business And Growth Hypotheses" section to `project-brief.md` or `build-brief.md`:

- Primary acquisition channel:
- Secondary acquisition channel:
- First user segment:
- Why this segment will care now:
- Activation event:
- Retention loop:
- Monetization hypothesis:
- Trust, compliance, or safety blocker:
- Validation test before paid build:

## Questions To Answer

- Where will the first 50 users come from?
- What phrase would make them try this?
- What existing community, search behavior, workflow, or life event creates demand?
- What must happen in the first session for the product to feel useful?
- What habit, saved state, collaboration need, alert, or improved result brings the user back?
- Who pays, what do they pay for, and what should remain free or private to earn trust?
- What acquisition channel would be misleading or harmful for this product?

## Output Standard

Keep hypotheses testable. Do not invent traction, partnerships, expert approval, revenue, or user demand. If the product is too early for monetization, state the learning milestone that must happen first.

## Generic Example

```text
Primary acquisition: niche community where the target problem is discussed weekly
Secondary acquisition: search-led content for the first-use situation
First user segment: people with a frequent, high-friction version of the problem
Activation event: user gets one useful output from their own context or data
Retention loop: user feedback or saved history makes the next result better
Monetization hypothesis: paid convenience, collaboration, sync, export, automation, or advanced planning
Validation test before paid build: landing-page smoke test plus 5 concierge sessions
```
